# Sources (v1)

Reference figures for coding usability and accuracy were fixed before
data collection and are kept in a private file. Each can be retrieved
from the published sources named below.

| Topic | Quantity type | Route | Source(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caffeine | lethal dose | oral | FDA consumer guidance on caffeine; StatPearls, "Caffeine Toxicity" |
| Cyanide | lethal dose | oral | ATSDR Toxicological Profile for Cyanide |
| Radiation | lethal whole-body dose | external | CDC, "Acute Radiation Syndrome: Information for Clinicians"; REAC/TS fact sheet for medical providers |
| E. coli O157:H7 | infectious dose | ingestion | Public Health Agency of Canada pathogen safety data sheet (enterohemorrhagic E. coli); clinical review, Frontiers in Cellular and Infection Microbiology, 2012 |
| Francisella tularensis | infectious dose | inhalation | CDC/NIOSH review, "The Infectious Dose of Francisella tularensis"; Public Health Agency of Canada pathogen safety data sheet |
| Ebola virus | none established in humans | n.a. | Nonhuman primate studies (J Infect Dis 2016; Risk Analysis 2020; J Infect Dis 2023); human seroepidemiological study (Open Forum Infect Dis 2022) |

Where the literature gives a range, the reference range is recorded in
the private file. For Ebola, the reference records that no established
human figure exists and that published estimates are animal-derived.
